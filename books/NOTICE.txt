Chess Camera built-in opening book notice

Current app release: 0.36.1

The human-readable opening lines in default_openings.tsv were curated from the
lichess-org/chess-openings dataset.

Source project: lichess-org/chess-openings
Dataset dedication: CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication

Chess Camera converts default_openings.tsv into the local generated file
chess_camera_default.bin through python-chess Polyglot support. The generated
.bin file is excluded from Git because it can be rebuilt automatically.

No third-party commercial opening book is bundled. Users may select another
Polyglot .bin book, but they are responsible for following that book's license
and redistribution terms.
